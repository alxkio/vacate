## Annual leave: service-year increases

Paid, calendar-year entitlement; distinguish **current-employer service** from **total recognized service**. [Guide pp. 8–9](https://www.inegsee.gr/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Odigos-Adeion.pdf#page=8); corroborated by the [Ministry’s annual-leave guidance](https://ypergasias.gov.gr/ergasiakes-scheseis/atomikes-ergasiakes-sxeseis/adeies-ergazomenon/).

| Milestone | Five-day week | Six-day week |
|---|---:|---:|
| First calendar year | Proportion of 20 WD | Proportion of 24 WD |
| Second calendar year | Proportional; rises to 21 WD annual basis at 12 months | Proportional; rises to 25 WD at 12 months |
| Third calendar year onward | Full yearly entitlement; 22 WD after 24 months | Full yearly entitlement; 26 WD after 24 months |
| 10 completed years with current employer **or** 12 total | 25 WD | 30 WD |
| 25 completed years of service/prior service | 26 WD | 31 WD |

The six-day column is source context only. The mock displays the five-day figures. In the VAC-6 Maya fixture, annual allocation is **25 WD: 8 used, 3 pending, 14 available (56%)**; it does not infer an employee's service tier.

**Logic to preserve:** calendar-year boundaries and employment anniversaries differ. The third year can begin before 24 months are completed; do not grant the second-anniversary increase early. Apply the relevant higher service tier, not cumulative additions. Recognized prior service can affect the basis for a new hire; do not reset everyone to 20/24. Count scheduled working days, excluding applicable holidays/non-working days. Proration, rounding, service evidence, partial schedules and historical rule versions require explicit policy decisions. Do not infer total service from the latest hire date.

### Sick leave — research and provisional mock behavior

| Type | Group | Selection | Allowance / rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sick leave | Care and health | ✅ Selected for VAC-5 | **OPEN QUESTION:** policy interpretation, eligibility, duration, pay, units/period and evidence. Display allowance/eligibility **To confirm**; no quota or percentage. |

**Research checked 2026-10-23; effective-date and applicability review OPEN.** The [Ministry's Civil Code 657–658 guidance](https://ypergasias.gov.gr/ergasiakes-scheseis/atomikes-ergasiakes-sxeseis/anypaitio-kolyma-astheneia/) describes employer salary protection after at least ten days' work: up to half a month in the first employment year, up to one month thereafter. It uses an employment-anniversary year, not a calendar-year vacation allocation, and describes pay adjustments involving insurance benefits. The [Labour Inspectorate overview](https://www.hli.gov.gr/ergasiakes-scheseis/ergazomenoi-ergasiakes-scheseis/dikaiomata-ergazomenoi-ergasiakes-scheseis/astheneia/) also distinguishes the initial three-day pay treatment. These pay-protection periods are **not adopted as a fixed annual leave quota**. See the [source register](../compliance/policy-sources.md).

**Authorized mock default:** selectable Sick leave and its example action; manually entered working-day quantity is illustrative only. Show personal-illness scope, unknown allowance/pay, a source link and an uncalculable balance; no percentage, allocated days, payroll calculation, diagnosis, certificate upload or inferred eligibility. Keep policy questions open for review. The catalogue now contains **30 entries / 29 requestable choices**. Discretionary unpaid leave remains outside the shortlist.
