# Personas and jobs to be done

**Status:** PROPOSED — validate through interviews.

| Persona | Context | Job to be done | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee | Needs time away from work | “Tell me what leave I can take and let me request it confidently.” | Can submit a correct request without HR support. |
| Team manager | Must maintain service/role coverage | “Approve or decline a request quickly, knowing who will be unavailable.” | Reaches a defensible decision in under two minutes. |
| HR/operations admin | Owns policy and administrative accuracy | “Set the policy once, answer fewer balance questions, and retain an audit trail.” | Reduced manual reconciliation and fewer support requests. |
| Organisation admin | Owns setup and access | “Set up people and access without accidentally exposing employee data.” | Can onboard the organisation and pass an access review. |

## Interview prompts

- Describe the last leave request that was difficult to handle. What made it difficult?
- Which people should a manager be able to see, and which must they not see?
- How do you determine entitlement and working days today?
- Which system must receive absence information, if any?
- What would make you trust a new system enough to import employee data?

## Clarifications needed

- Which job roles must be covered during leave, and who configures them?
