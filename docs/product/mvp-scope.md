# MVP scope

**Status:** PROPOSED

## In scope

- Organisation onboarding and tenant administration.
- Person and team membership administration.
- Configurable leave types, work schedules, public-holiday calendars, and balances.
- Leave request, validation, submit, approve, reject, cancel, and audit history.
- One configurable approval policy per relevant scope/leave type.
- Permission-aware team availability calendar.
- Email notifications; Microsoft Teams/calendar integration only after its MVP value is confirmed.
- Data export for an organisation administrator.

## Deferred

| Capability | Why deferred | Validation trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-organisation people and managed external teams | Changes tenancy, permissions, and commercial model | Three paid design partners cannot operate without it. |
| Multiple teams per person | Requires coverage, approval, and visibility rules | Confirmed customer scenario and approved membership model. |
| Job-role coverage rules | Requires scheduling and role-assignment design | At least three target customers cite it as a buying requirement. |
| Native mobile apps | Duplicates product delivery surface | Responsive web/Teams fails a measured manager-approval need. |
| Payroll integrations | Integration-specific support burden | Repeated demand for the same provider from paying customers. |
| Resource management | Separate product category | Absence-management activation and retention are proven. |

## MVP acceptance journeys

1. Admin sets up an organisation, holiday calendar, leave type, and approval policy.
2. Employee sees an explained leave balance and submits a valid request.
3. Correct approver sees only eligible requests and approves or rejects with a recorded reason.
4. Calendar renders only data permitted for the viewer.
5. Admin can export a person’s relevant data and inspect a decision audit record.
