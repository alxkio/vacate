# Repository review — 2026-10-20

## Finding

Vacate is a documentation-only repository: no application, build configuration, migrations or executable tests are present. The material is enough to identify a product direction but not yet a complete specification of the company's leave workflow.

## Context reviewed

- README, AGENTS.md, domain.md, TechChoices.md and LICENSE.
- Product charter, MVP, personas/jobs, metrics and evidence register.
- Domain glossary, scenarios and policy model.
- Compliance data map, policy sources and retention schedule.
- ADR guidance, HTML market research and GitHub research/evaluation/role prompts.

No accepted ADR existed before this review. The original technology draft and role prompts prescribe significantly more infrastructure than a company pilot needs. Reference domain material contradicts itself on one versus multiple teams, cancellation, balance formulas and approval permissions. The market report lacks a decision-grade evidence register. These are findings from repository content, not new competitor or legal research.

## Changes and decisions

- Recorded only the founder-confirmed company-first, subscription-intended, Angular/.NET 10 direction as accepted ADR-0001.
- Rewrote README and product charter around that direction; retained database, hosting and detailed scope as proposals/questions.
- Replaced the technology catalogue with one web app, one API and one database proposal. Preserved the original at docs/archive/technology-original.md.
- Reduced proposed MVP to setup, allocations, requests, a simple approval inbox, balances, calendar and audit/export. Added acceptance and company-isolation scenarios.
- Marked historical domain and role/research prompts so old instructions do not silently restore the broader scope.
- Kept personas, metrics, evidence, detailed domain and compliance documents as drafts. Their extra capabilities do not expand the current MVP. In particular, automatic approval, policy packs and outbox events described in those drafts are not accepted implementation requirements.

## Recommendations

Start with a complete leave journey and manual administration. Preserve correctness, server authorisation, company isolation and auditability. Defer integrations, automation and complex organisational models until the company process demonstrates the need.

The subscription goal requires company data boundaries now, but does not require a payment provider for the first internal release. The company is the first workflow test; broader market fit and pricing remain unverified.

The existing LICENSE identifies GPL-3.0. OPEN QUESTION: confirm the intended repository/distribution licence before external distribution or accepting contributions. No licence change or legal interpretation was made.

## OPEN QUESTION

The charter lists the blocking workflow questions: company size/jurisdictions, working schedules and leave units, approval/fallback rules, allocation/cancellation/carryover rules and identity access. Company details cannot be inferred from the earlier Greek market hypothesis.

Azure SQL is a plausible low-cost database candidate, not an unlimited free service. Hosting availability, actual usage and paid-operation budget remain unverified. Current provider references and allowance limitations are recorded in TechChoices.md, checked 2026-10-20.

## Verification

Documentation-only change. Reviewed the diff, checked relative Markdown links in changed documents and checked that the accepted ADR contains only founder-confirmed direction. No application tests can run because no application exists. Future implementation must satisfy the MVP acceptance journeys, including two-company and concurrent-balance scenarios; this review is not release approval.
