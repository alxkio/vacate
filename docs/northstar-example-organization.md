# Northstar Example organization

Fictional IT-services company used by the Vacate demo. The tree reflects the 12-person fixture in [VAC-6](./jira/VAC-6-company-personas-and-visibility-fixtures.md).

```text
Organization: Northstar Example
│
├── Department: Technology
│   │
│   ├── Team: Platform
│   │   ├── Person: Leah — Platform lead
│   │   ├── Person: Ravi — DevOps engineer
│   │   └── Person: Noah — Software engineer
│   │
│   └── Team: Applications
│       ├── Person: Maya — Software engineer
│       ├── Person: Theo — Software engineer
│       └── Person: Iris — QA engineer
│
└── Department: Business
    │
    ├── Team: Product
    │   ├── Person: Ella — Product lead
    │   ├── Person: Owen — Product designer
    │   └── Person: Zoe — Product specialist
    │
    └── Team: Operations
        ├── Person: Daria — Owner
        ├── Person: Nora — HR/operations
        └── Person: Sam — Operations coordinator
```

Departments and teams are shown as organizational groupings; job titles do not by themselves imply application permissions. This is a fictional example, not a production organization or authorization policy.
