## Annual leave deadline check — 2026-11-05

Official sources checked 2026-11-05: [Ministry working-time guidance](https://ypergasias.gov.gr/ergasiakes-scheseis/atomikes-ergasiakes-sxeseis/chronika-oria-ergasias/) and [Labour Inspectorate calculation/granting guidance](https://www.hli.gov.gr/ergasiakes-scheseis/nomothesia-ergasiakes-scheseis/adeies-ergasiakes-scheseis/etisia-kanoniki-adeia/imeres-ypologismou-kai-chronos-chorigisis/). Both support granting annual leave by the first quarter of the following calendar year; the Ministry explicitly identifies 31 March and cites Law 4808/2021 art. 61. The Ministry also describes payment consequences when leave is not granted. This is not evidence for silently deleting entitlement or granting software authority to settle payment.

The founder now selects 31 March inclusive as the GR product deadline, shown read-only in organization settings, with eligible previous-year-first consumption. [Canonical product decision](../product/product-charter.md#founder-decisions--submission-boundaries-and-yearly-charges--2026-11-05), [VAC-32](../jira/VAC-32-annual-renewal-and-carryover-processing.md). This supersedes the product's proposed configurable cutoff; the top-level lack of an approved complete statutory policy pack remains accurate. Exact provision commencement/later amendment coverage and employer-specific applicability remain UNVERIFIED; software timezone, post-cutoff and retroactive/cancellation handling are unresolved. No legal advice, automatic forfeiture or payroll engine is specified.
