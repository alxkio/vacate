# Retention schedule

**Status:** OPEN QUESTION — must be approved by counsel and customer contract terms.

GDPR requires storage limitation; it does not supply a universal retention period for absence records or audit logs. Record a purpose and legal basis for every duration.

| Record | Proposed handling | Decision needed |
|---|---|---|
| Active account and employment data | Keep while account/employment is active | Confirm controller/processor responsibilities. |
| Leave and balance records | Retain according to applicable employment/tax/dispute obligations | Obtain Greece-specific schedule before launch. |
| Audit records | Retain only as long as needed for security/accountability/disputes | Define access, minimisation, and duration. |
| Backups | Expire through a documented backup lifecycle | Define RPO/RTO and deletion propagation. |
| Closed tenant | Delete or anonymise after contractual and legal retention obligations | Define export, notice, and deletion workflow. |
